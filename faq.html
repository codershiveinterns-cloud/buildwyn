FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about cost, timelines, ownership and working with us.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a free scoping call we send a written proposal with a fixed price or a monthly rate, so you know the cost before anything starts.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused automation can take one to two weeks. A custom web application or SaaS MVP usually takes four to twelve weeks. Your proposal includes a timeline with milestones.

Who owns the code?

You do. Code is kept in your repository and services run in your accounts. On final payment, all rights in the deliverables are assigned to you, as set out in our Terms of Service.

Where is your team located?

Buildwyn LLC is registered in Wyoming, USA. Development is done remotely from India, with hours that overlap the US business day for calls and replies.

How do we communicate during a project?

Through a channel you prefer (email, Slack or similar), with a weekly demo and a short written update. Decisions are always written down.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We are happy to sign your non-disclosure agreement before you share confidential details.

How does payment work?

Fixed-scope projects are paid in milestones agreed in the proposal. Monthly retainers are billed at the start of each month. Invoices are issued by Buildwyn LLC in US dollars.

Can you work on an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a short review of the code and infrastructure, then propose fixes or improvements with clear estimates.

What happens after launch?

You can take over fully with the documentation we provide, or choose a monthly maintenance plan for updates, fixes and small improvements.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly. We reply within one business day.
