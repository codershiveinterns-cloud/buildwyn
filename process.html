How we work

A clear process from first call to handover

You always know what is being built, when it will be ready and what it costs.

  1. Step 01 · Day 1

    Free scoping call

    A 30-minute video call to understand your goals, users, current tools and constraints. We tell you honestly if software is the right answer.

  2. Step 02 · Days 2–4

    Written proposal

    A short document with the scope, what is excluded, milestones, timeline and either a fixed price or a monthly rate. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  3. Step 03 · Week 1

    Kick-off and setup

    We set up the repository and hosting in your accounts, agree on a communication channel and confirm the first milestone.

  4. Step 04 · Weekly

    Build and demo

    Every week you get a working demo and a short written update: what was done, what is next, and any decisions we need from you.

  5. Step 05 · Launch

    Testing and launch

    We test with your real data and users, fix what comes up, and launch together.

  6. Step 06 · Handover

    Handover and support

    Code, documentation and admin access are transferred to you. Optional monthly support keeps everything updated and running.
