Who we are

Buildwyn LLC is a US software company registered in the State of Wyoming. It was founded in 2026 by Devendra Singh Sankhla, who leads every project personally.

Our development work is done remotely from India, with working hours that overlap the US business day. That lets us offer senior attention, fast turnaround and fair pricing to clients in the US and worldwide.

The name says what we do: we build the software, and your business wins.
