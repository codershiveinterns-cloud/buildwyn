Engagement models

Two simple ways to work together

Fixed scope

Project

A defined deliverable with milestones and a fixed price agreed in writing before work starts. Best for new applications, automations and MVPs.

  • Written proposal and timeline
  • Payment split across milestones
  • Change requests quoted separately
Monthly

Retainer

A set number of hours each month for ongoing development, improvements and support. Best for evolving products and maintenance.

  • Agreed monthly hours
  • Shared priority list
  • Cancel any month with notice

Pricing depends on scope and is confirmed in your written proposal after the free scoping call.
